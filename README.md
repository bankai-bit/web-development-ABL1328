## Introduction

This project presents three website homepage designs created for
different purposes: a restaurant website, a movie information website,
and a gaming and esports website. Each website has its own layout,
content, and color theme.

## 1. Gold Restaurant -- Restaurant Website

**Purpose:** To introduce a restaurant, promote special offers, and
display popular food items.

**Main features:** - Restaurant name and navigation menu - Welcome
section and special offer - About the restaurant - Popular dishes with
pictures and prices - Reasons to choose the restaurant - Footer
information

**Design theme:** Orange and cream colors, with food cards and a
centered page layout.

![Gold Restaurant screenshot](images/gold-restaurant.png)

## 2. MovieZone -- Movie Information Website

**Purpose:** To introduce a movie website where visitors can discover
films and read reviews and ratings.

**Main features:** - MovieZone title and branding - Navigation links for
Home, Movies, Reviews, and Contact - Welcome section - Movie discovery
and review introduction - Footer information

**Design theme:** Dark background, red welcome section, white text, and
a simple navigation menu.

![MovieZone screenshot](images/moviezone.png)

## 3. GameZone -- Gaming and Esports Website

**Purpose:** To share information about popular games, esports
tournaments, player rankings, and gaming news.

**Main features:** - GameZone title and navigation menu - Welcome
section - Popular games such as BGMI, Valorant, and Minecraft - Upcoming
tournaments table - Top players leaderboard - Gaming news panels -
Footer information

**Design theme:** Dark background with purple accents, game cards,
tables, and news panels.

![GameZone screenshot](images/gamezone.png)

## Learning Outcomes

-   Understand the structure of a webpage, including headers, navigation
    menus, content sections, and footers.
-   Learn how images, cards, and tables organize website content.
-   Understand how colors and layouts can match a website's purpose.
-   Explore homepage design for different categories of websites.

## Conclusion

These three website designs show how webpages can be created for
different purposes. Gold Restaurant focuses on food and restaurant
information, MovieZone focuses on movies and reviews, and GameZone
focuses on gaming and esports. They demonstrate how layout, content, and
color themes can be selected to suit a website's topic.
